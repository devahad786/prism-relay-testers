# Mission: play Prism Relay on this phone

Read AGENT_BRIEF.md first. Its tools, screenshot rule, action budget, and the two
report files all apply. This file replaces the generic account-and-todo mission
and the `goal` line in personas.json. Still play as your persona: their patience,
reading language, and this device's size, text scale, and theme.

Prism Relay is a portrait puzzle. Package `com.techversol.prismrelay`. There is
no account. A fresh install opens on Title.

## Play this, in order

1. Title. Read every label. Tap **Continue** (starts the saved level; on a fresh
   install that is level 1).
2. Level. Select a piece by tapping it. Move it with **↑ ← ↓ →**. Turn it with
   **↶** and **↷**. **Undo** and **Restart** are there if a move was wrong.
   Try to finish the level. If you are stuck after 4 different attempts, tap
   **Hint** once and continue. A finished level opens a completion screen:
   **Next level**, **Replay**, **Level select**.
3. Open **Level select**. Switch to chapter **02** and start one level there.
   Leave it after a few moves. Do not play all 120 levels.
4. Open **Settings**. Toggle **Sound**, **Music**, **Vibration**, **Reduced motion**,
   and **Toggle larger text**. Leave the screen and open Settings again to see
   whether the toggles stayed. Then **Back**.
5. From Title, open **How to play** and **Privacy policy**, read them, then **Back**.

## What to look for on THIS device

Cut or overlapping labels, buttons under the gesture bar or off the board,
arrows too small to hit, a board that does not fit a tablet or a small phone,
text that stays tiny when the persona's font is large, and a toggle that does
not stick. A crash or a frozen screen after a tap is a blocker.

Unity draws some controls as pictures. If `screen.py` lists a label, tap that
centre. If a piece or arrow has no label, tap the centre you see on the screenshot.

## Report

Same files as AGENT_BRIEF.md. In the summary, name the device and say whether
level 1 was finished. Score whether you would keep playing on this phone.
